#+TITLE: Welcome to bl-ed

* What this is
An editor for =.bl.org= files, written in Beam Lisp and compiled to JavaScript
by the sync profile. Every key you press runs a BL function; the page only
draws what BL decides.

* Moving around
** By character and word                                              :basics:
=h j k l= move, =w b e= select by word, =x= selects a line, a count repeats
(=3w=). =v= toggles select mode, =Esc= collapses.

** By structure
=]h= / =[h= next and previous heading, =g u= up to the parent heading,
=]b= / =[b= next and previous source block. =space o= shows the outline.

** By form, inside a cell
Put the cursor in the cell below and try =m m= (select the form under the
cursor), then =g j= (first child), =g L= (next sibling), =g k= (parent).
=m i (= selects inside the enclosing form, =%= jumps to its partner.

#+begin_src beam-lisp :id greet
(defn greet
  "Say hello to `who`."
  [who]
  (str "hello, " who "!"))
#+end_src

* Editing
=i a I A o O= enter insert mode, =Esc= leaves it. =d= deletes, =c= changes,
=y= yanks and =p= pastes. =u= / =U= undo and redo. =C= adds a cursor on the
next line; typing goes to every cursor.

Structural edits work on the form under the cursor: =space r= raises it over
its parent, =space w (= wraps it, =space s= splices the enclosing form.

* The editor is a Lisp machine
Press =:= and type a form. It runs against the live editor:

- =(ed/outline)= lists the headings
- =(ed/goto 12)= jumps to line 12
- =(ed/find "cell")= selects every match
- =(ed/bind :normal "Q" :doc-end)= rebinds a key, right now
- =(ed/commands)= lists everything you can run

=space space= opens the command palette; =space k= then a key tells you what
that key runs.
