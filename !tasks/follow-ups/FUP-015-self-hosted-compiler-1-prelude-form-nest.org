** Detail

My compiler emits an EXTRA chain of intermediate bindings for the nested
destructure:

position threads the whole-var differently than the Elixir compiler's
rest_destructure + destructure_steps interaction (compiler.ex ~L2333
rest_destructure vs the general destructure_steps). The single-temp-vs-inline
decision for nested rest patterns differs.
