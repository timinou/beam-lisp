* Idea
A browser tab (or Worker) is a node:
- typed pids that cross the wire;
- links and monitors whose exit signals travel over the socket;
- a BEAM supervisor that can restart a client actor.

* Evidence already in hand
- research/js-actors/runtime.bl rebuilds the OTP signal rules in BL: atomic death (`rt-terminate!`); two exit deliveries, explicit vs link-carried (runtime.bl:1195-1260); names, call/cast, and a supervisor actor.
- Verified against native BEAM: 11 exit-signal reasons, 4 name/call rules, 25 table rules, 10 supervisor rules.
- Pids are already typed `[:pid runtime serial]` (open decision O-1 in research/js-actors-future/README.md).
- research/js-actors-future/03-boundaries-ets-ports-distribution.md; FEAT-054 (cross-Worker, design only).
