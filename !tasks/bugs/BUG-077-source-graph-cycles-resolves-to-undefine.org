* What is NOT established

The cause is NOT isolated. Prime suspect: a stale or partial compiled artifact for
`source-graph` in the build cache (`.local/bl/…`), produced or left by this
session's many `bl run -p priv/build …` invocations — i.e. an artifact predating
`cycles`. That would explain "defn present in source, var absent at runtime" and
why it appeared mid-session. I did not verify it, and I did not check whether
clearing the cache restores the var.

Also possible, and not excluded: a load-order/partial-load interaction, since the
namespace DOES load (other `sg/*` vars such as `header*`, `references` and
`closure-hash` are used successfully through the same alias in the same runs).
