#+title: Live logical backup retention and durable publication

* Contract
Optional BlobRetention serializes FS/ETS token pins with direct deletion,
destructive close and GC. Puts remain available. Exact tier identities join FS
aliases; stale release cannot clear newer pins, dead owners are pruned and
unsupported tiers refuse admission. Ephemeral close remains idempotent.
GC refuses before inventory; substrate deletion repeats the same fence.

publish-directory! fsyncs caller-owned immutable private staging, atomically
renames to a distinct canonical sibling with NOREPLACE, then fsyncs its parent.
Symlinks and existing destinations refuse. Work runs on an owned native thread
outside DirtyIo and accepted work survives caller death. Post-rename uncertain
outcomes preserve the visible artifact. This is not live-engine relocation.

its pinned directory/file descriptors and parent, then rechecks root identity.

symlinks and special files, and caps nesting at 256. This makes a verified
visible artifact durably reconcilable after an accepted publisher loses its
reply; visibility alone is never reported as stable storage.
