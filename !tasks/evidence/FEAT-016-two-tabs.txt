## Two REAL browser tabs, one server, own sockets

Tab A set to `product`, then left untouched for the rest of the run.
Tab B opened separately (window.open → its own LiveView socket) and
used to create a task.

  tab A before B wrote       4 tasks
  tab A after  B wrote       5 tasks     <- never touched
  tab A saw the new task     true
  tab A role after wake      product     <- kept its own identity

## The decision this feature turns on, verified live

ONE task — "written by the other tab" — as seen by each tab at the same
instant:

  tab B  (no lead chosen)    offers [doing, dropped]
  tab A  (product)           offers [dropped]

Two live pages, one row, two different answers. Each re-asked its own
question rather than rendering the writer's.

Had the broadcast carried a rendered board, tab A would be showing
[doing, dropped] — an action a product lead is refused for — which is
the defect commit 2107d67 removed, and it would only ever appear with
two tabs open as two different leads.
