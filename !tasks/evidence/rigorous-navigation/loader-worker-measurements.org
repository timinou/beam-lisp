* Contract
On a loader miss, host.loader asks an already-loaded diagnostics namespace for
an optional timing hook. It never loads telemetry during bootstrap. The hook
carries diagnostics alone to the worker and rebases its scope to the executing
Env, restoring previous state in finally. It does not convey caller publication
ownership or capabilities. Library loading retains its existing Env rules.

runtime.phase/completed separates loader-wait (queue plus execution elapsed in
the caller) and loader-worker (execution inside the loader). Both are inclusive;
nested requires can produce nested measurements. Do not sum them. Reductions
are sampled in each executing process; no worker work is charged to the caller.
Disabled or unavailable diagnostics emits no records or timing samples. Loaded
namespace fast paths do not construct a hook. Optional lookup on a miss has an
unmeasured cost; disabled-overhead acceptance remains pending.
