* Recovery and listener negative gates
The combined run reproduced five progress-observer recovery failures. Killing
the observer loses status and synchronous ticket retirement crashes the serial
executor. Listener lifecycle tests timed out and are not accepted evidence yet.
The harness exited and was reaped; these failures remain open work.

* Progress observer recovery implemented
Ticket retirement now sends a nonblocking cast to the observed PID. Losing the
optional observer cannot kill the executor after an effect has committed.
Admission failure reports =:outcome :unknown= and never retries the thunk.
A missing observer reports conservative busy/incomplete status; recovery attaches
idempotently, preserves tickets, and uses a generation-tagged FIFO barrier before
claiming complete knowledge of the queue. A completed ticket can briefly remain
visible until its retirement cast is handled. Queued is a lower bound during
recovery, not an exact measurement.

Validation: repaired Env/recovery/progress/context gate: 44 assertions passed.
Recovery/model and existing daemon snapshot gate: 26 assertions passed. Veritas
refutes and replays =[accept observer-dies]= for the old protocol; 500 seeded
histories witness the candidate. The real OTP gate kills the observer during a
blocked effect, confirms that the effect drains once without retry, and executes
the next request on the same sequencer PID. This does not claim exactly-once
execution across arbitrary VM crashes.

* Listener failure and explicit ownership implemented
A pristine occupied-port probe exited its caller with :eaddrinuse. This explains
the earlier Ward timeout: the test worker died through the startup link instead
of returning an assertion result. web/serve now traps startup exits in its own
bounded controller, conveys the submitting Env, links a successful listener to
the caller and retains its real OTP parent until listener shutdown. The parent
monitors caller death and shuts down the listener; unlinked-supervisor handoff
alone would not transfer proc_lib parent ownership.

live.app/serve checks the returned result, closes only a connection it allocated
when startup fails, and returns namespaced listener/ownership handles on success.
New stop! waits for listener shutdown before closing internally created shared
data. Caller-provided connections survive. Existing real HTTP/SSR tests now stop
their served apps. Ordinary app data still requires the explicit stop lifecycle;
this does not claim resource reclamation after an arbitrary VM kill.

Validation: 40 assertions pass across lifecycle, real HTTP around-wrapper and
SSR-style files. Includes actual occupied ports, idempotent stop, provided versus
owned connections, and killing a successful listener's caller. Earlier negative
probe and timeout remain recorded rather than counted as passing validation.
