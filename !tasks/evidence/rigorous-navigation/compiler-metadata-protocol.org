#+TITLE: Compiler metadata publication design probes
#+STATE: PROTOTYPE_VALIDATED

* Scope
research/navigation-next/metadata-publication-protocol-test.bl is a design probe,
not the integrated Env storage representation. It uses Veritas plus actual OTP
locks, ETS batches and killed processes. No compiler metadata contract changed.
loader-reentrancy-and-metadata-prototype.log: all 19 assertions pass.

* Results
Veritas refutes sequential replacement despite read exclusion while the writer
is alive, and replays its reduced counterexample. A writer may die after one row
changes; the next reader then sees a mixed table. The generation/batch candidate
is witnessed across 500 seeded histories of length at most 30. This is bounded
model evidence, not proof of all executions or authority for runtime adoption.

Actual global/trans nesting allows a competing requester into the still-running
outer operation after the inner trans returns. The proposed reentrant helper
retains exclusion, including a nested exception, and releases after outer return
or exception. Actual ETS publication with descriptor and generation entries in
one insert survives writer kill at the before/after barriers: only the complete
old or complete new table is read. Barrier waits have deadlines.

* Remaining gates
Integrate selected/full reads, namespace shadowing, AOT detach, undefinition and
replacement into the same protocol. Bound retired generations across repeated
interruption, test Env destruction races, measure lock overhead and scaling, then
regenerate the official host floor and clean packaged SDK. These probes alone do
not justify marking stage 2 complete.
