* Harness corrections

Second harness killed timeout's group but timeout had made its own child group;
lock prevented overlap and recovery refused75. Foreground timeout and explicit
lock-release gate now ensure one capped BEAM child at a time and reap all owned
processes. Failed logs retained. All artifacts are in home workspace.
* Boundaries
Actual process death/reopen, not SQLite modeling. This does not simulate disk
power loss or arbitrary torn sector writes. It proves safe invalidation/rebuild,
not reuse of a code certificate across VMs. Resuming an unchanged certified
snapshot without recopy still awaits durable closed emitted dependency evidence.
