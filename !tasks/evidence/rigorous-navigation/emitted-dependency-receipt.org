#+title: Bounded actual emitted dependency evidence

* Implemented
system.publication-closure reads published IR, follows same-namespace/core/sugar
helper bodies, reads actual global values, walks every clause/guard, and records
remote module/OTP identities. Local calls lower to remote shim calls; these
must include actual helper bodies rather than just the shim MD5.
Environment generation references become finite identity data before retention.
Opaque values, unknown providers/IR and dynamic apply targets decline. Bounds:
256 definitions,32768 nodes,2MiB evidence. Distinct entries are charged once;
the collector does not rewalk its whole retained graph for every IR node.

* Validation
closure-owner-final-gate.log: dependency15, profiler44, ownership9 and
connection19 assertions pass (87), four files green. Earlier failed logs show
resource-bearing generation rejection and missed source-shim helper edges;
they are diagnostics, not passing gates.

* Explicit authority limits
This is optional dependency infrastructure, unused by model reuse/cancellation.
Global publication epoch conservatively invalidates unrelated reloads. Other
namespace providers/re-exports decline. Remote implementations are pinned leaves;
their transitive host-call closure/effects are NOT proved. Actual socket/read
barriers, pure-effect proof, domain/work proof and closed durable certificates
are still required. Positive evidence explicitly exposes erlang/send; it does
not infer purity. Publication metadata-to-body binding is audited separately.
