callback sets and payload-dependent arithmetic require separate measurement.

do not remove them merely to quote the faster number. Admission now is not a
proof of reply/state compatibility or coverage of every future producer.

* Logic-module repair
The same VM evaluates exact pre-repair system.tier source and current source.
Two counterexamples reproduce old alpha=true/new alpha=false:
- structs with identical fields but DIFFERENT module tags;
- internal raw-stack versus public frame-list erlang:raise/3 nodes.
Both distinctions are executable meaning, not source annotation. The comparator
now includes the non-child semantic fields. Source/position annotations remain
ignorable. Native regression batch integration-10 passes12 new identity,
27 existing tier and10 SMT-tier assertions. This does not make the comparator a
complete compatibility prover: pattern binder alpha-renaming and body/reply/state
relations still need explicit support before authorizing general migrations.
