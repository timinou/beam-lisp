* The asymmetry as it stands

`spell.server/walk` accumulates every other effect as a by-product of
evaluation:

  (do! :op payload)    → :intents    recorded, performed by the host
  (push! @name v)      → :pushes     recorded, pushed after the reply
  (ask! text)          → :ask        recorded, started by the host

`read!` alone evaluates WITHOUT recording. That is correct as far as it
goes — a body must compute with the answer, and `spell.server`'s comment
on `read!` explains at length why a recorded read would force the caller
to merge results the body could not see.

But evaluating and recording are not exclusive. A read can do both.

* The change

In `spell/src/spell/server.bl`, the `read!` arm of `walk`:

    (= hd "read!")
    (let [op (walk (second form) env st)
          payload (walk (nth form 2 nil) env st)]
      (swap! st (fn [s] (assoc s :reads (conj (get s :reads []) {:op (name op) :payload payload}))))
      (perform-read (name op) payload))

Then `handle` and `handle-info` carry `:reads` in their answer alongside
`:intents`, and the `st` atom seeds `:reads []` like the other channels.

~5 lines of change plus the plumbing. The walker gains ZERO authority:
recording is exactly as pure as `:intents` already is. It still writes
only into its own state atom, still calls nothing it was not handed,
still cannot name a performer.

* Why this matters more than it looks

** `:topic` becomes derivable, so the contract clause can go

FEAT-016 added `:topic :board` to a contract's opts. It is written by
hand, and it is a SECOND statement of something the body already says:
the board contract reads `(read! :board {:role @role})` in both its
`:refresh` handler and its `on-info` clause.

With reads recorded, the host can subscribe a socket to exactly the reads
its mount event touched. The read name IS the topic — and it is already
registered, via `register-reader!`.

That is the same argument `topic`'s own docstring makes for putting it in
opts beside `:mount-event` rather than inventing a second spelling: one
need, one implementation. Here the need turns out to require zero
implementation, because the information was already in the body.

NB `:topic` must keep working — a contract that names one explicitly is
how a page subscribes to something it does NOT read at mount. Derivation
is the default, not a replacement.

** It subsumes FEAT-016's own leftover

The foot of FEAT-016 records: `@components` and `@leads` are computed on
every board read and rendered by nothing. `check.exs` reports it, and
`spell.machine/unrendered-assigns` finds it as a static heuristic.

With reads recorded, "an assign nothing depends on" becomes a DERIVED
fact over real data rather than a heuristic — and the same machinery
answers both "what invalidates this page?" and "what does this page
waste?".

** The dataflow graph becomes a value

Both registries already exist: `spell.server/readers` and
`BeamLisp.Spell.Server.performers/0`. This supplies the edges:

    intent :create-task → read :board → assigns @tasks @films … → 3 sockets

`spell.live-state` currently shows which vars the live environment holds.
This extends it to what depends on what.

And because `spell.run`'s four-rung ladder judges MODEL-PROPOSED
contracts, a proposal's dataflow being computable is a safety rung rather
than a visualisation: "this contract reads nothing and subscribes to
everything" becomes a refusable property.

* Acceptance

- A handler body with two `read!` calls answers `:reads` with both, in
  body order, with their walked payloads.
- A body with no `read!` answers `:reads []` — not nil, matching how
  `:intents` behaves for a body with no `do!`.
- `on-info` clauses record reads too (the board's `on-info` re-read is
  the case that matters).
- The walker still cannot perform a read it was not handed a reader for —
  an unregistered op still fails by name, unchanged.
- Every existing contract behaves identically: `:reads` is additive.
- Pure tests: no socket, no store, no browser. `spell.server/handle` is
  called directly, exactly as the existing intent tests do.
- `mix beam_lisp.test` green.
