* Ownership follow-up identified during inspection
BlobEts is opened by resolve-blobs inside the connection registry Agent action,
so its actual ETS owner can be the registry Agent, despite comments saying it
belongs to the caller. ETS source-owner death therefore does not prove blob,
writer or registry cleanup. An eventual owner reaper must cover all those edges,
respect adopted/heir-managed tables, stop on explicit release, and avoid treating
a durable handle's creator as its legitimate lifetime owner. Do not add a blind
Fjall creator monitor or claim a global/datalog GC fixed these ownership edges.
