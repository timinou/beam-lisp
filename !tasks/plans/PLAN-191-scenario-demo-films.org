#+title: Scenario demo films
#+status: IN-PROGRESS

* Outcome
A scenario's witnessed screens and recordings become a client-facing film.
Beam Lisp owns the authored film value, the scenario/run reference and export.
An optional Python/FFmpeg worker handles speech files and media encoding.
No browser, provider credential or media executable enters the core runtime.

* Stages
1. Add a declarative film contract and a duration-safe narration/subtitle export.
2. Add camera keyframes, cursor movement/clicks, chapters and real video sources.
3. Add an opt-in page/capture layer over the native browser port; exercise it.
4. Produce the Knowledger French review demonstration from witnessed histories.

* Acceptance
- Deepgram voice/model is explicit; credentials resolve only at the call.
- Every narration cue covers the complete measured speech interval.
- Audio cannot be cut off by a short visual or a caller's duration hint.
- Original scenario verdicts remain separate from presentation claims.
- Source frame/video hashes, voice, measured durations and editing decisions
  are retained in a manifest alongside MP4, SRT, VTT and chapter metadata.
- Tests cover timing, invalid inputs, camera/cursor projection and real export.
- Native capture uses isolated profiles and closes owned processes on failure.

* Visual direction for the first host
Knowledger's warm paper palette, restrained rust accent and clear typography.
The actual product occupies the frame. Short chapter labels orient viewers;
subtitles have a reserved band and never cover review controls. Camera moves
explain one detail at a time. French narration describes client actions.
