* Stage 7 — Safe warm startup and native acceleration
Two independent gates after measurement, not one default-on switch.
- Realm startup: realm-key code/AOT/search paths, exact build/project/protocol
  handshakes, atomic startup, idle caps, purge/drain. Test two conflicting projects,
  ambient environment, external host reads, reload, failed/parallel startup and
  stale artifacts before considering a shared warm default.
- Native calls: typed emitted argument/intermediate/overflow/work bounds, runtime
  guards, bounded native work and BEAM fallback. Differential boundary/bignum/
  adversarial-input/reload/timeout tests. Candidate eligibility alone is insufficient.
Deliverable: accelerate only measured hot kernels and demonstrably isolated startup.
