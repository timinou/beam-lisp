#+title: Canonical committed view: production integration audit
#+date: 2026-10-08

* Confirmed paths in merged main
- datom.conn/store-cell shares writer, basis, subscribers, blobs and lifetime,
  but does not share schema. connect-with constructs a new schema atom.
- db independently dereferences schema and basis; these are not an atomic view.
- transact-in-writer and load-in-writer publish basis and broadcast db-after.
- install-schema-in-writer transacts through a private schema shadow sharing
  the real basis, then performs coverage work, then resets only caller schema.
- sync-schema-map-in-writer follows the same separate publication pattern.
- excise-in-writer also advances basis after physical deletion and derived invalidation.
- reconnect-from-db constructs a schema atom from the supplied snapshot while
  reattaching current writer and basis. Old schema can therefore meet new basis.

* Required implementation boundary
A store-lifetime cell owns one immutable committed view containing schema,
basis and coverage authority. db dereferences that cell exactly once. Separate
legacy schema/basis fields may remain compatibility views but cannot authorize
queries or writes. Prospective writer connections carry a private view and do
not publish their intermediate basis or subscriber reports to the shared cell.
All acknowledged source commits publish one final view before subscribers.

* Durable schema changes
Schema metadata, tuple effects and coverage checkpoint must become durable
before publishing the final pair. Backfill runs in a hidden derived generation;
old readers retain their old authority, not a half-built index. Crash after
source commit but before publication requires reconstruction from committed
metadata. An in-memory pair fixes read tearing but does not prove durability.

* Validation gates
Sibling handles must agree on schema and ordinary entity/query projection at
one basis. Previously captured db values retain previous interpretation.
Cover connect, install, sync, ordinary transact, bulk load, excision, speculative with,
reconnect-from-db, writer restart and close/reopen. Kill the writer between
durable commit and final publication, and during derived staging. Compare
ordinary query oracles with and without optional indexes. Capacity refusal
must preserve source writes. Do not enable optimized schema-sensitive reads
until their coverage authority follows the same committed view.

* Scope guard
This audit is a concrete implementation map, not a claim the canonical view is
implemented. Changing only store-cell to share a schema atom leaves read tearing,
private-shadow basis publication and durable coverage boundaries unresolved.
