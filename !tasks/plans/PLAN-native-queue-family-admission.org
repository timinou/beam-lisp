generation fences under the native writer. Explicit conflicting protocols refuse.

are diagnostics, not opening authority. This also checks already-bound cached
queue connections before an opaque config factory, closing a memory-store bypass.
No Fjall writer/directory is implicitly opened by a family plan or producer.

per VM. Short shared capacity fence makes cross-application bounds strict.
Dead annotations remain explicit recovery obligations rather than silently
forgetting potentially outstanding jobs; durable artifact restoration/cleanup is
not implemented by this first-order VM-lifetime association table.
