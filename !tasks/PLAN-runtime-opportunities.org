** Next AOT batch: implementation boundaries confirmed by source inspection
=AOT.load_unit_in_snapshot= currently finds explicit defn names syntactically;
=AOT.unit_defs= then materializes =Env.ns_defs= and scans body-module identity
to include generated functions. =Env.selected_ns_definitions= already provides
coherent projection of a known name set. Therefore the proposed replacement is
a dynamically scoped compilation journal at successful definition publication,
capturing namespace/name pairs including macro-generated definitions. Nested
dependency loads must have independent journals and restore their caller's
journal in a finally block. Failed publications must not enter the journal.
The emitted artifact should project the union of explicit and recorded names.

Required negative controls: unrelated inherited definitions, two files sharing
a namespace, nested require, generated defn/multimethod/record helpers, repeated
definitions and failed compilation. Compare exported definitions, values and
initializer effects on cold compilation and artifact reload. Arbitrary
top-level effects are an unresolved packaging admission question; source
inspection alone does not establish their replay correctness. Do not admit
new packaging forms until those controls pass.
