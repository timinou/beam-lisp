Beam Lisp / Browser actor prototype

Send a message.
Watch BL receive it.

The actor and its mailbox policy are written in Beam Lisp, compiled from BL-ANF to JavaScript. This page only forwards strings into a module Worker and displays what the BL actor says back.

Starting Worker…
Watch the automatic startup and timer demonstration, then send your own messages. The actor waits indefinitely between events; Stop actor ends it. Run with python3 -m http.server 8765 --directory research/js-actors from the worktree root.
